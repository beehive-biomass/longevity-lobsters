## Working assumptions (correct me, then the copy follows)

- **Bee** — female creator, Bangkok. Her lane: the rave/nightlife scene
  crossing into recovery and long-game wellness. The brand metaphor she
  owns: **the molt** — lobsters grow by shedding; so do ravers.
- **Audience** — Thai-first, bilingual, 20–35, event-going, phone-native,
  LINE as the default messenger.
- **The product funnel is already built** (the seven screens): the gift →
  the gift is yours → join · LINE or your own key → intake → your shell →
  bring your numbers → me (dashboard). Bee's channels feed the first two
  screens; LINE carries everything after.
- **Thai copy here is draft-tight but needs Bee's native voice pass** —
  she should bend the particles and slang to how she actually talks.
  Copy marked 🦞 is the CTA spine and should stay recognizably itself.

## The one-line strategy

Give the gift first — **no account, no email, nothing to buy** — and let
the morning after the rave do the selling.
