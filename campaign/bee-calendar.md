## After D+14

The cycle restarts at the next event anchor. Between events, the standing
rhythm is the pillar ratio (4 night / 3 morning-after / 2 long-game /
1 shell per week) plus the LINE drip for each new cohort. Cohorts stack:
every event weekend tops up the same funnel.

## Review ritual (every Sunday night, 15 minutes)

- shortener clicks by placement — what earned the click?
- LINE friend adds by source — which surface converts attention into a
  relationship?
- best-performing piece: make its sequel for next cycle
- worst-performing ask: soften or retire it
