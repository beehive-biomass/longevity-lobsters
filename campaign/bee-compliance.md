# Thailand compliance & data notes for Bee's channels

Not legal advice — wording discipline that keeps the campaign on the safe
side of Thai consumer-protection and health-advertising norms, plus the
PDPA lines that make "your data, your keys" a promise, not a slogan.

## 1. Health wording — the gift is lifestyle, not medicine

The protocol copy lives at the intersection of wellness and health, which
is exactly where Thai advertising rules bite. Keep every claim in the
*lifestyle/wellbeing* register:

**Say**
- กลับตัว / ฟื้นตัว (come back / recover, everyday register)
- รู้สึกดีขึ้นในวันจันทร์ (feel whole by Monday)
- พักผ่อน แสง น้ำ การเคลื่อนไหว (rest, light, water, movement)
- "written from root-cause functional medicine" as *description of the
  author's lens*, exactly as the gift page itself phrases it

**Never say**
- รักษา / หาย / ป้องกันโรค + any disease name (treat / cure / prevent)
- any specific-disease claim, implied or testimonial ("หายจาก...", "cured my...")
- dosage-like supplement instructions (the gift explicitly contains **no
  peptides, nothing to order** — keep it that way in her content too)
- before/after medical framing (sleep-debt "numbers" talk is fine;
  clinical claims are not)

Rule of thumb for Bee: talk like a friend who read the manual, never like
a clinic. If a caption would fit a hospital pamphlet, cut it.

Nightlife content: 20+ framing only, no alcohol-brand glamorization in
the same frame as the gift CTA, and no shooting inside venues that
prohibit it.

## 3. PDPA (Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562)

The product's spine — your data, your keys — happens to align with what
PDPA made Bangkok audiences aware of. Use it honestly:

- **Collection:** LINE OA collects friend identity; intake collects
  self-reported answers. When product instrumentation is added, a plain
  notice at intake ("เก็บอะไร เพื่ออะไร ลบได้ทุกเมื่อ" / what we keep,
  why, deletable anytime) satisfies the spirit of notice-and-consent.
- **The promise must be true:** "ลบได้ทุกเมื่อ // deletable any time"
  only appears in copy if deletion actually works by then. Until the
  product ships deletion, the safe phrasing is "ข้อมูลอยู่กับคุณ ไม่ได้ขายต่อ
  // your data stays with you, never sold" — true today of a static site
  with no backend.
- **Broadcasts = direct marketing** under PDPA when they promote; every
  promo broadcast should carry an easy opt-out ("พิมพ์ว่า หยุด เพื่อหยุดรับ
  // reply STOP"). The drip's give-first rhythm keeps promo density low,
  which is both good manners and good law.
- Never paste a follower's identifiable info (name, avatar, health
  answer) into public content without explicit permission.

## 4. Testimonials & screenshots

Dashboard screenshots and "ตัวเลขของฉัน" posts: use her own data only, or
anonymized/mocked screens (the design boards double as mock data). If a
community member's result is shared, get it in writing first and show the
consent, not just the claim.
